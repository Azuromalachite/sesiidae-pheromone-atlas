# Changelog

All notable changes to the Atlas's records, schemas and IDs. A change that
alters a record shape or an ID is marked **breaking**. Curation detail is in
`docs/curation-log.md`; this file says what a user of the data needs to know.

Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).
Versions will follow semantic versioning once v0.1.0 is tagged. Until then,
nothing here is a release, and no DOI has been minted
(`docs/releases-and-occurrences.md`).

## [Unreleased] (0.1.0-dev)

### Added

- **Records:** 16 taxa, 19 references, 17 compounds, 11 genomes,
  252 observations and 6 searches. Every observation is from a primary full
  text, and every batch was re-read against the PDFs by an independent
  reviewer.
- **Record kinds and observation types:**
  - observation types `chemical_measurement`, `behavioural_response` and
    `electrophysiology`;
  - genome records with archived Ensembl Rapid Release gene sets and their
    MD5s;
  - search records, the only basis for "not found".
- **Experimental context on behavioural records:**
  - `trap`: design, colour, CIE L*a*b*, supplier;
  - `dispenser`;
  - lure `purity`, with `kind` (chemical / isomeric / both / unspecified)
    and `basis` (measured / stated).
- **Derived exports** (`derived/`):
  - one JSONL file per record kind;
  - the taxon × layer matrix;
  - `summary.json` and `SHA256SUMS`.

  They are generated by `scripts/build_release.py` and checked in CI with
  `--check`.
- **Release bundles:** `scripts/build_release.py --bundle VERSION`. Only
  their `manifest.json` carries the version, tag and commit.
- **Environment:** `requirements.lock.txt` (exact versions, resolved by
  `scripts/lock_requirements.sh`); `CITATION.cff`.
- **Validator checks:** `OBS_ID`, `ANNOT_ID`, `SEARCH_ID`, and the
  identical-comparator check in `OBS_FIELD_CONFLICT`, with a failing fixture
  for each.

### Changed (breaking, all before any release)

- **All IDs are `spa.`-rooted** (`spa.tax.`, `spa.cmpd.`, `spa.obs.`, …).
  Taxon IDs no longer carry a family prefix.
- **Observation IDs** are `spa.obs.<taxon slug>.<reference slug>.<what>`,
  derived from each record's taxon and reference. They were
  `<epithet>.<year>.<author>.<what>`, which was ambiguous for `bicingulata`.
- **Annotation IDs** carry the taxon slug. The old
  `spa.annot.<provider>.<method>.<geneset>` was shared by three species.
- **`compound_class` → `entity_type`** (`chemical` | `computational_model`).
  Occurrence and role (natural product, precursor, pheromone) come from
  observation and reaction records, never from the molecule.
- **`gland_composition` → `chemical_measurement`**, with a required `sample`
  (fixed `materials`, `material_as_reported`, and `result_scope`, which is
  `combined` when several materials are listed). `context.tissue` is removed.
- **`releases/` → `derived/`.** The taxon × layer matrix is generated, no
  longer hand-made.
- **`requirements.txt` → `requirements.in`** plus `requirements.lock.txt`.
- **Conventions:**
  - with `significance: not_tested`, `effect` is the direction of the raw
    numbers;
  - `identification` follows the authors' own wording.
