## How to attribute

Cite the Sesiidae Pheromone Atlas with the version or release you used and a
link to this repository. When a release has a DOI, cite the DOI.

## Third-party content

Some records will contain or derive from content of other resources (for
example UniProt, ChEBI or Rhea, themselves under CC BY 4.0). That content
remains under its own licence and its source is named in the record. The
Atlas licence covers the Atlas's own curation — normalisation, linkage,
evidence states and provenance — not the upstream material.

Facts reported in the scientific literature (compound identities, blend
ratios, accessions) are cited to their primary sources in each record.
