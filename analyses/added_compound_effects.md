# What does adding one compound to a lure do?

Generated by `analyses/added_compound_effects.py` from `derived/tables/` only. Do not edit.

**66 contrasts** whose treatment lure holds exactly one compound more than its comparator, in the same trap and dispenser, across 8 species: 50 are true **additions** (every compound of the comparator at the same amount) and 16 are **replacements** (the base was reduced, usually to keep the total dose), which answer a different question.

7 added compounds were tested in more than one species.

`share` is the added compound's percentage of the treatment lure when both lures use one unit. `not_tested` means the source ran no test on that contrast; the effect is then the direction of the raw means.
