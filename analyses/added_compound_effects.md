Generated by `analyses/added_compound_effects.py` from `derived/tables/` only. Do not edit.

7 added compounds were tested in more than one species.

`share` is the added compound's percentage of the treatment lure when both lures use one unit. `not_tested` means the source ran no test on that contrast; the effect is then the direction of the raw means.
