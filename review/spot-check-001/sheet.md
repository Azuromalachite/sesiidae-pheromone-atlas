Drawn by `scripts/spot_check.py draw` with seed 20261023: 24 observations at random from 524 (simple random sample over all observations, hard cases excluded from the pool), and 14 hard cases picked by hand.

For each record, open the paper, find the place named under *where in the paper*, and check each row against it. Write the verdict in `results.yaml`:
- `ok`: every row matches the paper;
- `error`: name the rows that do not, with what the paper says;
- `unclear`: the paper does not settle it; say why.

Record what you see, not what you think the curator meant. A record whose notes explain a reading is still checked against the paper.
